From the first edition to the thirtieth

Techfest began as a student idea in 1998. The renaissance theme is a nod to all the craft that came before the screens.

1998

The first Techfest. It set the shape the festival still has: competitions, lectures, workshops and exhibitions.

2008–09

iNexus goes abroad. The Nexus programme reached Sri Lanka, the UAE and Denmark.

2009–10

Scintillations lights up the night with evening exhibitions and shows.

2014–15

Lectures by A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, Bjarne Stroustrup and Vint Cerf, with a footfall of about 1.45 lakh.

2018

A Guinness World Record for the most LED lights lit simultaneously, set with SoULS, IIT Bombay.

2026

The 30th edition, An Aetherial Renaissance. Heritage meets futuristic technology, 16–18 December.
